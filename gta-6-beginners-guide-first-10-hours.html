GTA 6 Beginner’s Guide: First 10 Hours

Start with story missions before chasing every side activity. They unlock important systems and areas.

Early priorities

- Learn the map and travel routes - Build useful early cash - Save before risky missions - Explore gradually after story progress

Avoid overspending

Do not buy the most expensive vehicle immediately. Save for upgrades and useful equipment first.
